---
title: Community Adapters
---

In addition to the officially supported [Laravel](https://laravel.com/) adapter, the following
community-built server-side adapters are available:

- [AdonisJS](https://docs.adonisjs.com/guides/frontend/inertia)
- [ASP.NET Core](https://github.com/pmd-coutinho/dotnet-inertia-adapter)
- [Core PHP](https://github.com/webkul/inertia)
- [Django](https://github.com/inertiajs/inertia-django)
- [Echo](https://github.com/kohkimakimoto/inertia-echo)
- [Go (gonertia)](https://github.com/romsar/gonertia)
- [Go (inertia-go)](https://github.com/petaki/inertia-go)
- [Grails](https://github.com/matrei/grails-inertia-plugin)
- [Guren](https://guren.dev/docs/guides/inertia)
- [Mako](https://github.com/inventor96/inertia-mako/)
- [Phoenix](https://github.com/inertiajs/inertia-phoenix)
- [PSR (framework-agnostic)](https://github.com/maskulabs/inertia-psr)
- [Rails](https://github.com/inertiajs/inertia-rails)
- [Rust](https://github.com/stuarth/inertia-rs)
- [Rust](https://github.com/KaioFelps/inertia-rust)
- [Sails](https://github.com/sailscastshq/boring-stack/tree/main/packages/inertia-sails)
- [Symfony](https://github.com/skipthedragon/inertia-bundle) (Inertia v1)
- [Symfony](https://github.com/nytodev/inertia-bundle) (Inertia v2/v3)
- [WordPress](https://github.com/evo-mark/inertia-wordpress)
- [Yii3](https://github.com/maskulabs/inertia-yii)
